# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-14

## Context
We need to remember why decisions were made, for the team and for interviews.

## Decision
Use lightweight ADRs (this format) in `adr/` folders, reviewed by PR. Statuses: Proposed, Accepted, Superseded.

## Consequences
Small overhead per decision; clear history of trade-offs.
